# Insight promotion boundary

This integration adds no newly accepted market or performance insight. Current analysis must separate FACT, INTERPRETATION, HYPOTHESIS and GAP and identify the eligible and observed populations.

Future reviewed interpretations may be promoted here only with their exact evidence release, distinct reviewer, scope, expiry and prohibited extrapolations. A synthetic media/test pass does not validate competitor framing or video-model quality. A higher observed metric does not explain why a Reel performed.
