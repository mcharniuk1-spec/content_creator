# M2-S05-08 — Give the workflow a useful failure state

M2 Builds · English · 50 seconds · 9:16 · **Candidate; independent and owner review pending.**

**Viewer task:** Route an uncertain draft back to its process owner. **Friction:** A workflow labels an output finished even when a necessary source fact is absent. **Human decision:** The owner resolves missing information and rechecks the corrected draft.

## Three openings

1. We gave this workflow a draft it should reject.
2. A useful workflow can tell you why it stopped.
3. What happens when the next step is missing a fact?

## Spoken script

We gave this workflow a draft it should reject. It is a small local test, using a fictional meeting note and a deliberately faulty action list. The draft adds an owner and a deadline that the source never supplied. Our check flags both fields and changes the result to needs human review. It does not send anything. The useful part is the reason: these details are unsupported. The process owner can now resolve them. After a correction, check again. Do not hide the failure by calling the original output finished. This catches the two problems in our fixture. It is not a test of every possible AI error. Pick one missing-input case in your workflow. Decide who sees it and what happens next.
