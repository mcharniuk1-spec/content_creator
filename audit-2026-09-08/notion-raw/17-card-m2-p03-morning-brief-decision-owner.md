## The idea in one minute
**For:** Team lead receiving a daily project summary.
**Problem:** A summary repeats information but leaves the decision, evidence, and responsible person unclear.
**What this video gives the viewer:** Turn the brief into a decision aid by keeping the source, question, and owner together.
**Why this approach:** The conflicting shipment dates provide a concrete example of why a shorter summary is insufficient. The resolution belongs to a person who can verify the source.
Builds · Regular publication · Original synthesis of several references · about 60 seconds, 130 spoken words.
## Spoken script
Your morning summary has twelve updates. Which one needs you? For a first test, put fictional project notes into three columns: what changed, what needs a decision, and what can wait. AI could group repeated notes and prepare a short draft. Ask it to keep a source beside each item. Now add a decision owner. A line saying 'delivery date unclear' needs a person who can confirm the date, not another summary. If two notes disagree, keep both visible. Write the question that would resolve the difference. Before acting, the owner checks the source, makes the call, and records it. That turns the brief into something the team can use. Open one summary you already receive. Add 'decision needed' and 'owner.' How many lines actually ask somebody to do something?
