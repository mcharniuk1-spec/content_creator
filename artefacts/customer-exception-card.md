Rules:
1. A new request is a proposed policy, not a favour: either it goes on the card with an end date, or it becomes standard for everyone, or it is a polite no.
2. The onboarding questionnaire asks the common ones up front (knock or don't knock, gate, pets, parking), so most exceptions never become surprises.
3. What AI may do: draft the card from your past messages with that customer. What stays yours: deciding which exceptions the business keeps.
