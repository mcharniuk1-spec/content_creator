# Reels-first governed Signal-to-Studio architecture

## Context

The Content Engine must adapt the newer M2Lab Radar research layer into a reliable system while keeping creative production original and owner-governed. The local and Notion Reels populations are unreconciled, current multimodal evidence is incomplete, and earlier YouTube analysis is no longer an active market corpus.

## Decision

Instagram Reels is the sole active research and Studio-evidence platform. The system has two operational engines and one shared control plane:

1. **Signal / Research** owns approved acquisition, immutable observations, typed attempts, data quality, scope eligibility, versioned metrics, transcript/comment/shot/scene evidence, uncertainty, Account aggregation, and independently reviewed decision releases.
2. **Studio** owns approved M2Lab/ArchFlow context, original concepts and ScriptCards, semantic scenes and shots, A-roll/B-roll/split-screen plans, assets, captions/audio/edit instructions, previsualization, review, and separately gated production.
3. **Control plane** enforces immutable release identity, explicit denominators and state axes, maker/reviewer separation, clean-room transfer, stop rules, receipts, and staged projections. It is governance, not a third content engine.

`signal-to-studio.v2` is the only permitted bridge. It transfers accepted functions, evidence summaries, uncertainty, limitations, prohibited uses, and approved brand context. It does not transfer mutable working tables, Notion rows, creator scripts, wording, footage, likeness, music, distinctive layouts, or shot sequences.

## Consequences

- YouTube is archive-only and cannot influence current ranking, prevalence, creator recommendation, or ScriptCard evidence.
- Local and Notion populations remain separate until stable identity export and reconciliation pass.
- Notion is a redacted owner cockpit; it is not an analytical database or knowledge authority.
- Project-local WikiLLM-compatible notes plus immutable run evidence form the durable knowledge layer; external Obsidian/Nexus writes remain separately owner-gated.
- LlamaIndex is optional and disabled until it beats the deterministic lexical baseline on a separately approved benchmark without citation, privacy, freshness, contradiction, or rights regressions.
- Foundation acceptance may use synthetic seam proof. Evidence-release acceptance requires current, rights-cleared, independently reviewed real Reels evidence and approved brand/market context.
