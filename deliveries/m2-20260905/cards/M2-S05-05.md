# M2-S05-05 — Let AI prepare the reply, then stop

M2 Teardowns · English · 50 seconds · 9:16 · **Candidate; independent and owner review pending.**

**Viewer task:** Prepare a draft response to a routine customer request. **Friction:** A draft can make a promise that the business has not approved. **Human decision:** A team member checks facts and confirms whether anything can be promised or sent.

## Three openings

1. Your AI can draft the reply without making the promise.
2. Put the human check before the customer sees the answer.
3. A routine question should not become an automatic commitment.

## Spoken script

Your AI can draft the reply without making the promise. Imagine a customer asking when something will arrive. Use a fictional request for the first test. Give the assistant only approved facts and a clear instruction to flag anything it cannot confirm. In this example, the draft promises tomorrow. Our approved information does not support that date. The next step is a person checking the request, not the message going straight to the customer. That person corrects the draft, confirms any promise, and decides whether to send it. Keep the boundary visible: prepare, review, then send. A missing fact returns to the process owner. Map one routine reply today. Mark the exact point where a human must decide.
