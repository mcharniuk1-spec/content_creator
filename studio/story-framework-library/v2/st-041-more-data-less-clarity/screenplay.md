# ST-041 — More Data, Less Clarity

Truth state: `ORIGINAL_LOCAL_PREVISUALIZATION / EXTERNAL_PROVIDER_NOT_RUN`

## Logline

A research lead must make one decision despite additional sources arrive without synthesis, because volume can obscure the decision; the story resolves when less material supports a clearer choice.

## Story spine

- Setup: a crowded evidence wall
- Inciting change: The paper stack changes state before the objective is secure.
- Objective: make one decision
- Obstacle: additional sources arrive without synthesis
- Stakes: volume can obscure the decision
- Action: notice the paper stack → groups sources by the question they answer → inspect the decision lens
- Proof: decision lens
- Limitation: Omitted sources remain in the ledger.
- Payoff: less material supports a clearer choice
