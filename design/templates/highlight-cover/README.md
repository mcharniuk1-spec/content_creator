# highlight-cover

1080x1920, placement `highlight-cover`. Paper surface, circle cropped by Instagram to
r 486 at (540, 960). Designed to hold at the smallest reported display diameter, 110 px,
so shape carries it and nothing is meant to be read at that size.

| Slot | What it takes |
|---|---|
| `mark` | `on` places the mono Ink mark, 620 px, centred in the circle. Use for the first highlight. |
| `word` | One or two short words, Display 144 on 1.03, up to 2 lines, left aligned in the square inscribed in the circle. About 9 characters a line. |
| `numeral` | A digit or two, Display 400, same box origin. For numbered highlights. |

Use exactly one of the three per cover. The mono Ink mark is used rather than the
colour mark because at 110 px displayed the mark is about 63 px wide, below the 32 px
colour floor where the Signal wedge turns to mud.

Geometry: mark box 620x620 centred at (540, 960), corners 438 from the centre. Word box
684x297 and numeral box 684x412, both centred on (540, 960) and left aligned at x 198,
the square inscribed in the crop circle. Text is vertically centred in its box, so a
one-line word sits on the circle's centre line.
