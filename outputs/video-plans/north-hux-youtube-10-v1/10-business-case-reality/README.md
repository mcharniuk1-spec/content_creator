## Spoken script

Your automation ROI is probably missing four costs. Time saved is only the first line. Subtract integration, review, exceptions, and change adoption. Then adjust the benefit by actual usage and error cost. Pilot one workflow with one owner, one baseline, and one outcome metric. Scale only when the workflow and customer outcome improve—not when the demo looks impressive.
