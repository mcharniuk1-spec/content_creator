# ST-024 — Bridge of Evidence

Truth state: `ORIGINAL_LOCAL_PREVISUALIZATION / EXTERNAL_PROVIDER_NOT_RUN`

## Logline

A research narrator must connect a claim to a conclusion despite one supporting plank is missing, because the conclusion cannot bear weight; the story resolves when the bridge carries only the supported claim.

## Story spine

- Setup: a paper bridge model
- Inciting change: The bridge changes state before the objective is secure.
- Objective: connect a claim to a conclusion
- Obstacle: one supporting plank is missing
- Stakes: the conclusion cannot bear weight
- Action: notice the bridge → adds dated sources and labels the remaining gap → inspect the source plank
- Proof: source plank
- Limitation: More sources do not automatically remove bias.
- Payoff: the bridge carries only the supported claim
