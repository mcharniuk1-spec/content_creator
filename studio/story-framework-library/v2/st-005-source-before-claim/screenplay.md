# ST-005 — Source Before Claim

Truth state: `ORIGINAL_LOCAL_PREVISUALIZATION / EXTERNAL_PROVIDER_NOT_RUN`

## Logline

A research editor must turn a claim into cited explanation despite the draft has no evidence parent, because the audience could mistake opinion for fact; the story resolves when the claim becomes bounded and inspectable.

## Story spine

- Setup: a source library
- Inciting change: The magnifier changes state before the objective is secure.
- Objective: turn a claim into cited explanation
- Obstacle: the draft has no evidence parent
- Stakes: the audience could mistake opinion for fact
- Action: notice the magnifier → traces the sentence to a dated source → inspect the source map
- Proof: source map
- Limitation: One source may still be incomplete or contested.
- Payoff: the claim becomes bounded and inspectable
