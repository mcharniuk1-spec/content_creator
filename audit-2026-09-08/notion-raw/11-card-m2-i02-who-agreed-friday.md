## The idea in one minute
**For:** Owner or operations lead reviewing customer replies.
**Problem:** A fluent draft can turn an unknown delivery date into a promise the team never agreed to.
**What this video gives the viewer:** Separate drafting from committing. Show the missing fact and the person who must resolve it.
**Why this approach:** Open on the contradiction between the note and Friday. Reveal the correction on screen before explaining the wider M2 approach.

Teardowns · Profile introduction · Original synthesis of several references · about 60 seconds, 127 spoken words.

## Spoken script
This reply looks ready. But who agreed to Friday? Imagine a customer asks when an order will arrive. The note says the team is checking. The draft says Friday. At M2 Lab, we look for moments like this before connecting AI to everyday work. Draw a line between preparing a reply and making a promise. AI can work from approved facts; missing facts need a person. In this example, replace the invented date with a question for the order owner. Keep the customer's original message beside it. The useful output is an editable draft with the gap visible. Sending stays a separate decision. Take one message your team repeats. Underline every promise in the reply. Can you point to the fact and the person behind each one?
