## The idea in one minute
**For:** Business owner evaluating an automation proposal.
**Problem:** A smooth demonstration leaves the difficult input and ongoing responsibilities unanswered.
**What this video gives the viewer:** Ask for one awkward test and record the remaining access, cost, maintenance, and ownership questions.
**Why this approach:** The missing deadline is a specific challenge a viewer can bring to a supplier. A small success remains evidence about that example only.
Teardowns · Regular publication · Original adaptation of a reference pattern · about 60 seconds, 136 spoken words.
## Spoken script
Someone offers to automate your whole workflow. Ask to see the awkward case first. Choose one repeated task, such as turning enquiry notes into a draft response. Bring a fictional example with a missing detail. Ask what goes in, what comes out, and who checks it. Then ask what happens when that detail is missing. Does the system stop, ask a question, or quietly guess? Watch the answer in the test instead of filling it in yourself. Before agreeing to a wider build, get the open questions in writing: access, running costs, maintenance, and responsibility when something breaks. A successful small example answers a small question. Keep the unanswered ones on the page. For your next AI proposal, add one line: show us how this handles our difficult example. What would you put in that example?
