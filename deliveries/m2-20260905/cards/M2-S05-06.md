## Spoken script

Free software still needs someone to own the workflow. Suppose a tool prepares your weekly update. The software price is only one box on the checklist. Check whether it needs hosting, paid model calls, setup work, or access to team information. Then name the person who checks the output and the person who handles a failed run. For this first test, keep the result as a draft. A failure should not publish an update. We are not comparing prices here. These are questions to answer before choosing the setup. A simple tool with clear ownership may suit your task. You still need to test that fit. Before installing anything, fill in three fields: running needs, reviewer, and failure owner.
