# ST-011 — Why the Number Dropped

Truth state: `ORIGINAL_LOCAL_PREVISUALIZATION / EXTERNAL_PROVIDER_NOT_RUN`

## Logline

A business analyst must diagnose a visible drop despite three plausible causes compete, because the wrong intervention wastes the next cycle; the story resolves when one cause remains plausible.

## Story spine

- Setup: a review room
- Inciting change: The chart changes state before the objective is secure.
- Objective: diagnose a visible drop
- Obstacle: three plausible causes compete
- Stakes: the wrong intervention wastes the next cycle
- Action: notice the chart → compares the affected segment against the rest → inspect the segment comparison
- Proof: segment comparison
- Limitation: The comparison narrows causes; it does not prove one.
- Payoff: one cause remains plausible
