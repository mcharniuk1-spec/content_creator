## Knowledge layers

| Layer | Contains | Must not contain |
|---|---|---|
| Source evidence | URLs, dated metadata, transcript/caption observations, rights and freshness | unsupported visual claims, copied creative expression |
| Observation | what a source demonstrably does at transcript/metadata/frame level | causal performance explanations |
| Classification | controlled tags and evidence confidence | free-form “viral style” labels |
| Interpretation | clean-room reusable structure such as problem → mechanism → proof | source wording, distinctive characters, branded UI |
| Story design | original protagonist, objective, obstacle, action, proof, limitation and payoff | an unclosed hook or decorative B-roll |
| Previsualization | stateful frame sequence, continuity bible, shot/camera motive, prompts | claims of final production readiness |
| Experiment | owned variants and predefined metrics | post-hoc winner stories or cross-account causal claims |
| Durable memory | independently reviewed constraints and repeated patterns | raw transcripts, frames, comments, metrics or prompts wholesale |

## Stable invariants

1. The unit of construction is a meaningful event, not an arbitrary cut interval.
2. Every story needs an identifiable POV, concrete objective, obstacle, stakes, observable action, changed target, proof, limitation, payoff and handback.
3. Every frame must answer: what changed, why now, and what must the viewer inspect?
4. B-roll receives one declared narrative job; footage without information gain stays out.
5. Technical details follow a ladder: concrete problem object → mechanism verb → visible state change → proof object → boundary.
6. Preserve a continuity anchor across cuts: actor, target object, position, motion vector, audio bridge or semantic color.
7. Do not collide dense caption reading with the most complex visual reveal.
8. The hook opens a precise answerable gap; payoff closes or honestly bounds that same gap before CTA.
9. Analysis describes source function, not source expression. Change semantic premise, causal topology and visual mechanism for clean-room output.
10. Structural validation is not performance evidence.
11. A Studio Card may consume only an accepted immutable Signal release plus an owner-approved Brand Evidence Pack; current Reels diagnostic rows and the Notion projection are not sufficient inputs.
12. Keep competitor transcript, footage, likeness, distinctive shot order, music, and layout out of the creative seam; transfer functional principle, evidence boundary, risk, and owned-test design only.

## Ten story families

`in_medias_res`, `result_rewind`, `diagnostic_mystery`, `failed_attempt_correction`, `object_metaphor`, `countdown_ladder`, `before_after_transformation`, `proof_reveal`, `contradiction_resolution`, and `process_handback`.

Use the family as reveal order, not as the entire idea. Topic, creator archetype, audience job, emotional arc, proof type, scene topology and visual operation must be selected separately.
