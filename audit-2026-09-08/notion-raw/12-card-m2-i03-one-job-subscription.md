## The idea in one minute
**For:** Team lead choosing an AI subscription.
**Problem:** Tool comparisons become feature lists before the team has agreed what a useful answer must do.
**What this video gives the viewer:** Introduce M2 Radar with one customer-service job and four observable acceptance checks.
**Why this approach:** The same fictional question and checklist make the comparison legible. This is an evaluation method, not a recommendation for an untested vendor.

Radar · Profile introduction · Original synthesis of several references · about 60 seconds, 127 spoken words.

## Spoken script
Before your team buys another AI subscription, write down one job it needs to do. Suppose customer questions arrive in three places. The job is getting each person a checked answer using the current policy. At M2 Lab, our Radar videos look at tools through tasks like this. Make a short checklist: use the supplied policy, show missing information, leave the answer editable, and wait before sending. Now try two options with the same fictional question. Put their drafts side by side with that checklist. Which answer can your colleague check and correct? What setup would the team have to maintain? Those questions belong in the comparison too. Keep the checklist when the tool names change. What is one requirement your next subscription would have to pass?
