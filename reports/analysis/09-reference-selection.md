# 09 — Reference selection

Spec §19, §21D, §76. Per selected hypothesis: three to six reels, each with one named function, the verbatim beat that makes it useful, the real beat and scene ids, the performance behind it, and what is borrowed structurally against what changes for M2's positioning. 41 references across the 10 selected hypotheses, 27 distinct reels, all in `data/analysis/hypothesis_refs.json` and in the `hypothesis_refs` table; the ingest reports 41 ingested, 0 rejected.

**Sources.** The 40-reel candidate pool in `reports/analysis/06-creators-and-references.md` §6, plus the `video_features`, `beats`, `scenes` and `creator_stats` tables of `data/radar.db` and the per-video analyses in `data/analysis/transcripts/*.json`. Every number in the performance column is read from `video_features` at generation time; none is typed by hand.

## 1. Rules applied

**R1 — Consistent creators may contribute several structural references; high-variance creators only their statistically meaningful reels.** 115 of 132 creators are HIGH_VARIANCE and only 17 are CONSISTENT (I-23). Every reel used below clears the meaningfulness floor (view_lift ≥ 0.5 or robust_z ≥ 1.5); the lowest are Db9MT-axxcO at 1.62x / robust_z 1.08 and Dbi-yxNgrhG at 5.85x / 1.45, both of which are used for forwarding behaviour rather than for reach. Exactly one CONSISTENT creator exists in the pool — damini.knows, DcoE5ZsK4I7 — and it is used in H-12 and flagged there as the most trustworthy row available.

**R2 — A multiplier is not comparable across reliability labels.** Reels by CONSISTENT creators score *lower* on creator-relative metrics (median robust_z 0.60, view_lift 0.65) than reels by HIGH_VARIANCE creators (1.37, 2.38) despite identical script shape, because both metrics divide by the creator's own spread. Wherever a large multiplier rests on a tiny author median — DUJYKENjZc5 at 388x on a 1 374-view median, DVZGBgTk6zz at 304x on the same, DZLDk9ySi-7 at 51x on 3 421 — the reason field says so and the reel is used for cadence or ordering, never as evidence that the idea travels.

**R3 — Gated and ungated rates are not the same quantity.** The comment-keyword gate moves comment rate x23, save x2.8 and share x1.8 while moving reach not at all (I-12), and 33 of the 40 pool rows carry one. The seven ungated rows were weighted up accordingly and six of them are used: DNA7-d3o5sQ and Dc_TX1CttIc (none), DbVKVz0y8xo and DTCOU9DkRTG (question_to_audience), DZ-wujrTJ0u (follow), DX9kWTYzssE (dm). Where a gated reel is used, the reason states which of its numbers can be read at face value.

**R4 — A reel may serve several hypotheses only with a different function.** Nine reels are used twice, always with two different functions, and the second use says so explicitly. Enforced as an assertion at generation time: no (code, function) pair appears twice in the file.

**R5 — Prefer small explainable sets.** Four references per hypothesis, five for H-11, drawn from 27 distinct reels rather than from the whole pool. Each hypothesis carries at most one reference per function family so that the shot plan inherits one clear instruction per slot.

**R6 — Never use a reel flagged unsafe or unusable.** Three reels were excluded outright on content grounds (§4).

**R7 — The quote is verbatim from the beat text.** Every quotation below is copied from `beats.text` for the beat id given, ASR errors included; where our own analysis records a mis-transcription ('Kenwa' for Canva, 'cloud code' for Claude Code, 'Chachi BT' for ChatGPT), the transformation column says that the structure is borrowed and the wording is not.

## 5. What the reference set is weak on

**One CONSISTENT creator in 27.** DcoE5ZsK4I7 (damini.knows) is the only reel in the whole selection from a creator whose output is predictable. This is a consequence of how the pool was built — creator-relative metrics systematically favour high-variance accounts — not a choice, and it means most of the multipliers quoted above are statements about a creator's spread rather than about the reel. Read the share and save rates, not the lifts.

**Twenty-one of the 27 reels carry a comment gate.** Their save and share rates are inflated by a known factor (x2.8 and x1.8) that reach does not share. Six ungated reels carry the functions where the rate actually matters — hook, explanation, proof, cta, a_roll, b_roll — and the gated reels are used mostly for cadence and transition, where the number is not the argument.

**No reference shows a failure, because none exists.** Across 266 analysed reels, `before_after` is the second-rarest proof type (n=5) and not one reel anywhere shows the author's own build breaking. Four of the ten selected hypotheses (H-11, H-12, H-13, H-18) turn on exactly that, so for those the reference set can supply the grammar and never the precedent. That is the intended gap: it is the only thing in the selection that no measured competitor can copy back.

**Nine frames per reel.** Every scene id quoted above comes from a nine-sample interpolation with `boundary_reason = 'sampled'` and low boundary confidence. A scene listed as running 44 seconds may have been four shots. Use the scene ids to locate the moment, never to plan a cut.
