# 07. Messaging hierarchy

Seven levels, from the one sentence that never changes to the line a single post
uses once. Use the lowest level that fits the surface. Never put a lower-level
line where a higher one belongs.

Levels 3 to 7 are operating detail. They are not in the brand book; they are
handled separately.

## 1. The promise

> **M2 Lab helps non-technical small-business leaders turn AI confusion into clear, useful everyday work, one workflow at a time, without coding or chasing every new tool.**

Verbatim from `01-positioning.md`, long dash replaced with a comma per that
file's punctuation rule. Use this line whole, or not at all.

## 2. Supporting line

> **Understand what matters. Start with one repeated workflow. Keep a human in control.**

## 4. Highlights (5)

Not in the brand book; handled separately.

| Name | Contents | Create when |
|---|---|---|
| `Start here` | Who we are, what we do, why to follow. 4-5 Stories. | Week one |
| `Teardowns` | The best process breakdowns. | After the 3rd Teardown |
| `We tried` | Tool tests, what broke. | After the 3rd Build |

| `Behind` | How we work, filming, the process behind the process. | Once material accumulates |

Covers follow the highlight-cover template in the design system: one surface color,
the mark or a large number, nothing else. Consistency across the row matters more
than any single cover.

## 5. Pinned posts (3)

1. Who we are and what this account is. Faces, roles, the promise. A 30-40
   second Reel.
2. The strongest Teardown. Proof that we think like implementers.
3. The strongest post by forwards. Set later, once real data exists.

The first two are prepared in advance. The third is swapped in after roughly a
month, once a post has earned the slot on its own numbers.

## 6. Broadcast channel

Not in the brand book; handled separately.

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Name | `M2 Lab — the workshop` |
| Description | `The full version of what doesn't fit in a reel: workflows, prompts, the tools we actually use, and what broke this week.` |
| First message | `This channel is the long version.` / `Reels show the result. Here you get the whole thing: the workflow file, the prompts, the part where it didn't work, and why.` / `One message a week. If it ever stops being useful, leave. We'd rather have 200 people who read than 2,000 who don't.` / `First one drops [day].` |

Prompt patterns that work in the channel:

- "What process in your work eats the most time and nobody has automated?"
- "Which AI tool did you try and quietly stop using?"
- "Name a task you think should NOT be automated."

Leave answers open 24 hours. A reply to someone else's answer posts back into
the main channel; that is what brings people back.

## 7. Where each level is used

| Surface | Which lines appear |
|---|---|
| Instagram Name field | Name field only. |
| Instagram bio | Bio, primary or reserve. Never the full promise. |
| Instagram highlights | Highlight names only. No message copy. |
| Pinned posts | Supporting line, adapted to the post. |
| Broadcast channel | Channel name, description, first message. |
| Reel caption | Supporting line, adapted to the specific episode. Never the full promise. |
| Reel closing line | The next-step instruction for that episode, per `04-voice.md` Principle 5. Not a hierarchy line. |
| Threads / TikTok bios | Their own approved bio text (see `source/profile-copy.md`), not this file's Instagram bio. |
| Partner or sponsor pitch | The promise in full. |
| Press or outreach one-liner | The promise in full. |
