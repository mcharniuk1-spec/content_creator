# ST-001 — Deadline Brief

Truth state: `ORIGINAL_LOCAL_PREVISUALIZATION / EXTERNAL_PROVIDER_NOT_RUN`

## Logline

An operations lead must turn a scattered brief into one decision despite three conflicting notes, because the meeting starts before the choice is clear; the story resolves when one accountable next step.

## Story spine

- Setup: a quiet project room
- Inciting change: The clock changes state before the objective is secure.
- Objective: turn a scattered brief into one decision
- Obstacle: three conflicting notes
- Stakes: the meeting starts before the choice is clear
- Action: notice the clock → sorts the notes by decision owner → inspect the decision receipt
- Proof: decision receipt
- Limitation: The receipt proves routing, not business success.
- Payoff: one accountable next step
