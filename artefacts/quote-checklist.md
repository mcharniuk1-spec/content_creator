# The lines a quote forgets

An 18-tech HVAC shop, eight years in, cleared 8 % on a job that looked profitable. The quote forgot four things. This is the list to run before any quote leaves.

- [ ] Drive time, both ways, at the real hourly cost
- [ ] Rental equipment, even for one day
- [ ] Consumables and materials that are not on the parts list (refrigerant, fasteners, sealant, bags)
- [ ] Warranty callbacks as a percentage of labour, from your last 12 months, not zero
- [ ] Permits, disposal, parking, tolls
- [ ] Waiting time you cannot bill (access, other trades)
- [ ] A second person reads the quote before it goes out
- [ ] Incidentals line: +10 % until your own data says otherwise

Then use the data you already have: pull your last 50 jobs, compare quoted vs actual for each line above, and write the average miss next to each box. That number becomes the default in the next quote. The model may fill the template from a photo or a call; a person owns the numbers.
