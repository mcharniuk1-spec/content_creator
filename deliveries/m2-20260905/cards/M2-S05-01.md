# M2-S05-01 — Choose the first task before the tool

M2 Teardowns · English · 50 seconds · 9:16 · **Candidate; independent and owner review pending.**

**Viewer task:** Choose the first manageable AI-assisted task for a small team. **Friction:** Tool selection begins before the team agrees on a task or a useful output. **Human decision:** A process owner selects the task and accepts the draft.

## Three openings

1. Your first AI project might fit on one sticky note.
2. Before choosing an AI tool, name the task that keeps coming back.
3. Which small task could your team test without changing everything?

## Spoken script

Your first AI project might fit on one sticky note. Pick something your team repeats. For this example, turning weekly notes into a draft update. Write down what goes in, what should come out, and who checks it. Give AI the drafting step. Keep the decision about what to send with a person. Now try one made-up example. Include a missing detail and see how the draft handles it. If it guesses, change the instruction: mark the gap instead of filling it. That gives you a small test you can inspect before connecting real work. Take one repeated task. Name its input, its output, and the person responsible.
