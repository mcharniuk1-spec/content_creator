# Historical knowledge retained outside the active Reel trace

The August YouTube calibration and 10K census are immutable historical research. They are not current competitors, English-qualified Reels, transcript coverage, or evidence for the current ranking. Their raw data and runtime remain in their original frozen runs, not imported into the new Instagram reconciliation database.

## Retain as method

- Separate observed evidence, interpretation, hypothesis and gaps. A large screened corpus is not a large qualified strategy sample.
- Keep source rights, transcript availability, actual decoded frames, comments and statistical eligibility as separate denominators.
- Use specific jobs, constraints, mechanisms and proof in scripts. "AI saves time" without a named task and check is too generic.
- Treat visual patterns as editorial hypotheses until tested on the active audience. Storyboard frames are plans; they are not generated clips or measured workflow proof.
- Preserve maker/reviewer separation, source-linked claims and reversible local replay.

## Retain from early Instagram work

The 91-record export was a limited handoff with 89 word-bearing records. Later local collection produced over a thousand machine outputs; the old export was reused in later reports without importing the larger store. Neither the older export nor the later output count certifies acoustic accuracy.

Semantic audits found generic mechanism labels, truncated CTAs and a decision claim transferred between Reels. The restricted checkpoint accepted only 34 annotations with limitations and quarantined 12. This explains why more records alone did not improve the cards: extraction, interpretation and writing needed independent quality control.

The stronger reusable implementation is the local provenance/diagnostic framework plus the partner's practical Hiker collector, operational database and production layouts. Preserve both; choose one versioned analytical release rather than allowing competing dashboards to silently select different sources.

## Do not carry forward as current truth

Historic "winning" durations, caption conventions, hook bans, mixed-language category rankings, causal-sounding associations and generic internal-system cards need revalidation. Historic tool/backend readiness and price assumptions also expire. Use the new English-only business-role contract, then remeasure a relevant cohort.
