## The idea in one minute
**For:** Small-business owner or team lead rebuilding a weekly customer update.
**Problem:** Repeated admin hides a more important problem: nobody has defined the source, reviewer, or acceptable result.
**What this video gives the viewer:** Show where AI could draft within an owned, checkable process. M2 is introduced through a useful task, not a broad promise.
**Why this approach:** The notes-to-update transformation makes an abstract adoption question visible. The unknown date gives the viewer one concrete quality check.

Builds · Profile introduction · Original synthesis of several references · about 60 seconds, 136 spoken words.

## Spoken script
Still rebuilding the same Friday update? That is a useful place to start with AI. We're M2 Lab. We help small-business teams work out where AI fits, and what still needs a person. Imagine three project notes becoming one customer update. Start with the notes, the reader, and the decision they need to make. Then map the steps: collect facts, draft the update, check it, send it. Give each step an owner. AI could help with the draft. The person checking it needs the original notes, including anything that is missing. Try one fictional example first. Does an unknown delivery date stay unknown? Can you trace every sentence back to a note? Start with one repeated task. Write down its source notes, its reviewer, and the mistake that would matter. Which weekly task would you test?
