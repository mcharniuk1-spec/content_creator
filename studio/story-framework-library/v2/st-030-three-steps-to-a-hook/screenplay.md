## Logline

A scriptwriter must open with an honest curiosity loop despite the first line is broad and abstract, because the audience cannot see a reason to continue; the story resolves when the opening question matches the answer.

## Story spine

- Setup: a writing desk
- Inciting change: The three cards changes state before the objective is secure.
- Objective: open with an honest curiosity loop
- Obstacle: the first line is broad and abstract
- Stakes: the audience cannot see a reason to continue
- Action: notice the three cards → names the concrete problem, stake, and promised mechanism → inspect the hook test
- Proof: hook test
- Limitation: Avoid certainty beyond available proof.
- Payoff: the opening question matches the answer
