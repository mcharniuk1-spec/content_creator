## Selection and writing procedure

1. Compare a Reel with its own account's available median and report the denominator. Keep comparison-cohort rank separate from the baseline count. Use a high view index to prioritize inspection; also inspect weaker examples when they provide a useful contrast. Neither ranking nor shared visual features establishes why a post performed.
2. Read the entire selected transcript and inspect timestamped visual evidence before assigning hook, problem, mechanism, demonstration, transition or CTA functions. A regular sample, cut candidate and semantic scene are different objects. Do not infer an unseen opening from a later presenter frame or assume the final sampled frame contains a CTA.
3. Write one viewer problem and one proposed solution. For M2, make the repeated task visible, identify the required inputs, bound what AI may draft or classify, retain the human decision, and offer one first test. Give a named tool only a role the source evidence and proposed workflow actually support.
4. Introductions should make positioning concrete through a task: a Friday update, disputed deadline, subscription decision, awkward enquiry or customer handoff. Regular publications should deliver a usable check: an appointment exception, a customer question, a decision owner, an awkward test case or a failure column. These are the slate's editorial choices, not proven audience preferences.
5. For a synthesis, combine mechanisms rather than stacking topics. Preserve one causal thread: the visible symptom, the missing rule, a small example, the human check and a next action. Bind each borrowed function to its own reviewed reference; the resulting problem, fixture, wording and framing must be original.
6. Give each scene an information job. A-roll states the judgment; B-roll makes the relevant input, missing field or changed state legible. Label fictional demonstrations. Show what changed and why before moving on. Keep the written fixture and exact spoken script authoritative when a generated composition guide contains shorthand.
7. Draft the body before choosing among three openings. Pay off the selected hook, remove throat-clearing and unsupported claims, read the speech aloud, and estimate duration from the actual word count. Seven scenes and sixty seconds are this slate's plan, not a universal retention formula. Apply the secondary handbook and humanize-writing pass after primary evidence and positioning, then independent review.

HYPOTHESIS: Showing an ordinary operating mistake and a small test may make M2's process-first positioning easier for an SME viewer to recognize. Future owned-post results can test that hypothesis. Do not present it as measured retention, reach, conversion or a market-wide trend.

Acoustic accuracy, complete-corpus visual interpretation, current global trends and future publication performance are unmeasured. Shooting, recorded speech alignment, editing QA and publication are subsequent stages.
