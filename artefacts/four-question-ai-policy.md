# The four-question AI policy (one page)

Adopted by: · Owner: · Last reviewed:

1. **What goes in.** Which data may be pasted into an AI tool at all. Client names, order ids, financials, health or HR data: no, unless the tool is on the company account with a data-processing agreement. Public text and our own templates: yes.
2. **What is sanitised.** Before pasting: names replaced, ids removed, numbers rounded. Who checks: the person pasting.
3. **Where a human checks.** Anything that goes to a client, to a customer, or that carries a number or a date is reviewed by a named person before it leaves. Drafts are drafts.
4. **What if it is confidently wrong.** It will be. The person who sent it owns it, the tool does not. Report it in the shared channel the same day so the next person does not repeat it.

Two lines for everyone: you may use AI to start, never to finish; if you would not paste it into an email to a stranger, do not paste it into the tool.
