# ST-020 — The Unreadable Proof

Truth state: `ORIGINAL_LOCAL_PREVISUALIZATION / EXTERNAL_PROVIDER_NOT_RUN`

## Logline

A content designer must show evidence legibly despite the full dashboard is shrunk into one frame, because the proof becomes decorative; the story resolves when the proof can be inspected on mobile.

## Story spine

- Setup: a phone-sized canvas
- Inciting change: The phone changes state before the objective is secure.
- Objective: show evidence legibly
- Obstacle: the full dashboard is shrunk into one frame
- Stakes: the proof becomes decorative
- Action: notice the phone → crops to one datum and keeps context in narration → inspect the cropped evidence
- Proof: cropped evidence
- Limitation: Cropping must not remove relevant context.
- Payoff: the proof can be inspected on mobile
