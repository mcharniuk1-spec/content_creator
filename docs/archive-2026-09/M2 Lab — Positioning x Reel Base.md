# M2 Lab — Positioning x Reel Base

**Working positioning hypothesis**

> **M2 Lab helps AI-curious SME commercial leads turn their marketing into more customers and less wasted effort — without adding headcount, coding, or chasing every new AI tool.**

This is a test lens, not a final public promise. The audience is the person accountable for the handoff from marketing activity to a real sales conversation. Their title can be founder, CEO, Head of Marketing or Sales Lead; the job is the same.

---

## 1. What the existing radar base can and cannot tell us

**Base analysed:** 2,363 English-language Reels, snapshot 1 September 2026.

| Cluster in the existing tags | Unique Reels | What it gives M2 |
|---|---:|---|
| Lead generation, scraping and CRM + AI in a concrete business process | 88 / 2,363 (3.7%) | Direct source material for the new positioning: inquiries, qualification, follow-up, CRM and missed calls. |
| AI content production + tokens / cost / limits | 150 / 2,363 (6.4%) | Adjacent material: content efficiency and tool cost can be reframed as “less wasted effort”, but only when connected to acquiring a customer. |
| Everything else | 2,125 / 2,363 (89.9%) | Mostly mechanics to study, not themes to take. Coding, AI-video spectacle and generic agent demos do not serve the new promise. |

### Conclusion

The base contains enough material to start, but it was collected around the broad category **AI in business processes**, not specifically marketing-to-sales work. Therefore:

1. Use the existing base to borrow **hooks and production mechanics**.
2. Do not treat it as proof that this exact audience has already been validated.
3. From the next radar run, tag and score a separate **Customer acquisition** track.

---

## 2. The new editorial boundary

### A reel fits only when it answers one of two owner questions

1. **How do we turn more of the marketing effort we already make into real customer conversations?**
2. **Where are we wasting people’s time before a customer gets an answer or a next step?**

### It must show one of these moments

`market insight -> message/content -> inquiry -> qualification -> owned next step -> follow-up -> proposal`

### It does not fit when it is only about

- making content faster, with no route to a customer;
- scraping thousands of contacts or bulk cold outreach;
- an AI agent that “closes sales” by itself;
- generic CRM/tool tutorials;
- tools, benchmarks or news with no change in the above chain;
- retention, support or accounting. They may become later verticals, but are not part of this first test.

### Proposed 12-post block mix

- **M2 Builds: 4** — public proof and trust.
- **M2 Teardowns: 5** — transferable owner pain and saves/shares.
- **M2 Radar: 3** — current hooks, always translated into a customer-acquisition decision.

This is deliberately more Teardown-heavy than the old 2 / 2 / 1 weekly split. The new positioning needs people to recognise the problem before they care about a tool.

## 6. New radar rule to add after this decision is approved

Add **Customer acquisition** as a first-class tag. A reference qualifies only when it has:

1. A recognisable SME pain at the marketing-to-sales handoff.
2. A specific stage: research, message, inquiry, qualification, next action, follow-up or proposal.
3. A transferable decision that does not depend on bulk outreach or a miraculous conversion claim.
4. An M2 angle: more customers **or** less wasted effort, with a human approval point.

### Selection questions for every future card

1. Would a commercial lead recognise this as a reason customers are being lost?
2. Does the post improve the path to a customer, not merely create more AI output?
3. Can M2 show a real test, boundary or failure rather than repeat the tool demo?
4. Is the benefit specific enough to forward to the person who owns marketing or sales?
