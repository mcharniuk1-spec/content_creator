# ArchFlow Content Context Capsule

Status: bounded starting context; refresh from the allowlisted parent run artifacts before any public claim.

## Pillar 1 — governed AI onboarding and knowledge reliability

### FACT

- The current concept helps one defined role complete one meaningful first task using bounded, source-grounded company context.
- It distinguishes retrieval from authority: an answer should expose sources, ownership, freshness, conflicts, and abstention/escalation state.
- PM and Engineer onboarding tracks remain separate because their first tasks, evidence, risks, and reviewers differ.
- The operator retains review and action authority. The system is not an employee clone or an autonomous replacement.
- Current public planning focuses on product-led B2B SaaS teams around 30–75 people. Older broader company-size ranges are historical context, not automatically current.

### INTERPRETATION

High-value content should make a visible operating failure concrete: conflicting sources, senior-team interruption, document dumping, key-person departure, generic role training, or a confident answer without authority. The resolution should be a bounded mechanism such as source-owner-freshness mapping, role/task packets, a decision trail, contradiction handling, citations, abstention, or approval state.

### HYPOTHESIS

An operations-first story showing one painful onboarding moment and one inspectable control will resonate more with product and engineering leaders than generic “AI transforms onboarding” language.

### GAP / prohibited claim

Do not claim validated ROI, a production customer outcome, universal two-week implementation, a current price, autonomous employee replacement, or guaranteed time-to-productivity without fresh approved evidence.

## Pillar 2 — Hotel Revenue Control

### FACT

- The current system is a local hotel-revenue demonstrator with synthetic/imported data, deterministic simulation, advisory recommendations, and operator approval/readback controls.
- PMS paths and catalog entries are simulated or staged unless a separate connector receipt proves configuration, authentication, fixture parsing, write, and readback.
- Public rate/availability signals are not semantic proof of a named competitor hotel's occupancy.
- The current design direction is an evidence/control grid for Home, Analytics, and Optimization surfaces.
- No live hotel writeback, production deployment, licensed compset occupancy feed, customer outcome, or validated revenue lift is established in this capsule.

### INTERPRETATION

Strong content can expose the difference between an impressive recommendation and a reliable operating decision: input freshness, source authority, protected bookings, policy bounds, approval, idempotency, execution receipt, readback, mismatch, and rollback.

### HYPOTHESIS

Hotel operators and revenue leaders may respond to concrete control failures—stale inputs, false occupancy inference, opaque recommendations, “adapter” versus live connector truth, and exception reconciliation—when each is paired with a visible advisory/operator-control mechanism.

### GAP / prohibited claim

Do not claim live PMS integrations, autonomous repricing, real competitor occupancy, production AI action, actual customer usage, quantified revenue impact, or validated demand. Treat the active V2 implementation as changing until its own independent final review completes.

## Shared content voice

- calm, exact, operational, and source-aware;
- problem and pain before features;
- one primary claim and one viewer job per asset;
- show the limitation and operator boundary;
- replace futurism, “10x,” “viral,” and autonomous-agent hype with inspectable states and receipts;
- every video has a useful static companion;
- references inform patterns, never copied expression.
