## The idea in one minute
**For:** Operations lead considering enquiry-drafting automation.
**Problem:** A clean demo conceals the missing and conflicting details that make everyday enquiries hard.
**What this video gives the viewer:** Introduce M2 Builds through three deliberately different inputs and a clear pass condition.
**Why this approach:** Three notes create a visual progression: complete, missing, contradictory. The last one tests whether the draft exposes a decision rather than silently making it.

Builds · Profile introduction · Original adaptation of a reference pattern · about 60 seconds, 128 spoken words.

## Spoken script
A perfect example tells you very little about a messy workday. Say you want AI to help turn enquiry notes into follow-up drafts. Make three fictional notes before you try it. One has every detail. One has no deadline. The third has two different deadlines. At M2 Lab, our Builds start with a small test like this. Decide what a useful result would look like before running it. Facts should stay unchanged. Missing details should stay visible. Conflicting dates should become a question for the person handling the enquiry. Compare the draft with the original note. A tidy paragraph still fails your test if it quietly chooses a date. Try the awkward cases before connecting the workflow. What is the missing detail that catches your team most often?
