# ST-010 — The One-Line Outcome

Truth state: `ORIGINAL_LOCAL_PREVISUALIZATION / EXTERNAL_PROVIDER_NOT_RUN`

## Logline

A technical writer must compress a complex workflow honestly despite the opening promises too many outcomes, because the viewer cannot predict the lesson; the story resolves when the hook and payoff match.

## Story spine

- Setup: a drafting desk
- Inciting change: The pencil changes state before the objective is secure.
- Objective: compress a complex workflow honestly
- Obstacle: the opening promises too many outcomes
- Stakes: the viewer cannot predict the lesson
- Action: notice the pencil → removes claims until one mechanism remains → inspect the scope bracket
- Proof: scope bracket
- Limitation: Compression must not delete limitations.
- Payoff: the hook and payoff match
