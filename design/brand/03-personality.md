# 03. Personality

Four traits. Every publication should be recognizable as ours by these traits, with the handle hidden.

## 1. Evidence-first

We say a thing only after we have run it, measured it, or read the primary source.

- We are the ones who show the test. We are not the ones who report the news.
- In content: a Radar episode about a new model opens with the one task we pointed
  it at, and the sentence "here is what changed and here is what did not".

## 2. Translator

We convert AI capability into a step of somebody's actual working day.

- We speak the language of the process owner. We do not speak the language of the
  release notes.
- In content: instead of naming a context window, we say how many support tickets
  fit in one pass and what still has to be split by hand.

## 3. Builder in public

We publish the working version, the trade-off and the part that failed.

- We are a lab with an open notebook. We are not a studio with a showreel.
- In content: a Builds episode names the step where the agent broke and shows the
  screen where it broke, before the verdict.

## 4. Human in control

Every workflow we publish names who reviews the output and who owns the process.

- We build assistance with an approval step. We do not build autonomy.
- In content: every Teardown ends with a step marked as human-owned and a sentence
  explaining why that step stays human.

## The visual formula

`Human face + real proof + one labeled verdict`

M2 Builds and M2 Teardowns carry all three. M2 Radar carries at least two. "Real
proof" is our own artifact: our screen, our workflow file, our fragmentation, our
number, never somebody else's demo. "One labeled verdict" is the Verdict Card on
screen, readable with the sound off, as set out in `04-voice.md`.
