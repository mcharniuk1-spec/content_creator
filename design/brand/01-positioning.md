# 01. Positioning

## Purpose

M2 Lab exists because non-technical business leaders are told that AI changes
everything and are never shown what to do on Monday morning. We take one real
repeated work process, build a controlled AI workflow around it, test it, and
publish the honest verdict. Michael builds the case, Max builds the thing.

## Public positioning statement

> **M2 Lab helps non-technical small-business leaders turn AI confusion into clear, useful everyday work — one workflow at a time, without coding or chasing every new tool.**

Typographic note: this is the approved wording. When it is set in published copy,
replace the long dash with a comma so it matches the brand's punctuation rule.

## Supporting line

> **Understand what matters. Start with one repeated workflow. Keep a human in control.**

## Internal operating statement

Not published. It decides what we make.

> **We help the person responsible for AI adoption in a small business choose one repeated workflow, break it into fragments, and build the first human-controlled version of it.**

## The M2 method

`repeated workflow -> fragmentation -> AI boundary -> human review -> first test`

Every piece of content, every offer and every future product reinforces this chain.

## What M2 Lab is

- A practical AI adoption media brand.
- A translator between AI capability and a real repeated work process.
- A builder in public, showing tests, trade-offs, failures and human-review boundaries.
- A future education, diagnostic and scoped implementation business.

## What M2 Lab is not

- A generic AI news account.
- A tool-list or prompt-hack account.
- A "make money with AI" account.
- A sales, marketing, finance, legal or HR expert brand.
- A promise to automate every business process.
- An "AI employee" vendor selling autonomous systems without process ownership
  and human approval.

## Credibility boundary

We can credibly claim to:

- explain AI to non-technical people at a beginner-to-intermediate level;
- break a repeated process into steps, inputs, outputs, decisions and risks;
- assess where AI can assist and where a human stays responsible;
- research tools and market signals;
- build and test a scoped workflow;
- document what worked, what failed and what should not be automated.

We stay silent on, and never claim:

- guaranteed revenue, conversion, retention, cost savings or productivity gains;
- advice in a business function where we have no evidence or expertise;
- a reliable solution for every reviewed tool or workflow;
- a replacement for professional legal, tax, medical, HR, credit or security judgment;
- AI that makes external decisions or takes actions without human approval.

Internal builds are experiments, not client case studies. Say "we tested", "we built",
"this broke". Say "client result" only after a real client pilot with an agreed
baseline and metric.

## Language and geography

**Language.** Public content, website, offers, workshops and cohorts are English only.
Keep one language per account.

**Geography.** Name no country in the public positioning during the first 60 days.
The content audience is non-technical, English-working small-business leaders.
The initial commercial focus is English-working SMEs in Europe. Netherlands and
Ireland are research and outreach hypotheses, not brand identities. Say "for Dutch
SMEs" or "for European businesses" only after real local conversations, pilots, or a
repeated country-specific problem.
