## The idea in one minute
**For:** Owner or reception lead responsible for appointment communication.
**Problem:** A reminder may complete the sender's task while leaving the recipient unable to request a change.
**What this video gives the viewer:** Introduce M2 through both sides of the handoff: customer action and staff responsibility.
**Why this approach:** Move from the customer's phone to the staff response path. That change of viewpoint makes the incomplete process tangible.

Teardowns · Profile introduction · Original adaptation of a reference pattern · about 60 seconds, 140 spoken words.
