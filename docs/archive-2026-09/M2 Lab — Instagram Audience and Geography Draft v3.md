# M2 Lab — Instagram Audience and Geography

**Status:** Revised working draft. It supersedes the geographic recommendation in v2 for the first Instagram test. It does not remove the UK, Netherlands or another country as a future commercial hypothesis; it removes an artificial country claim from the early content positioning.

## 1. The decision

Do **not** publicly position M2 Lab as UK-focused, Netherlands-focused or worldwide in its first 12–24 Instagram posts.

The account cannot reliably send an ordinary organic Reel only to one country. Meta describes unconnected Instagram recommendations through a viewer’s interests and prior interactions with similar content, plus predicted actions such as viewing, liking, saving and providing feedback. Geography can be one contextual signal — for example, locally popular media in Explore or a location tag — but it is not a creator-controlled country-targeting switch. [Meta on unconnected recommendations](https://ai.meta.com/blog/ai-unconnected-content-recommendations-facebook-instagram/), [Instagram Feed system card](https://ai.meta.com/tools/system-cards/instagram-feed-ranking/), [Meta Engineering on Explore](https://engineering.fb.com/2023/08/09/ml-applications/scaling-instagram-explore-recommendations-system/)

Therefore a country belongs in M2 Lab content only when a post genuinely contains that country’s case, market norm, collaborator, event, law, price or local business context.

## 2. Two layers, not one false target

### Editorial audience now

> **English-speaking or English-working SME owner-operators in Europe who are curious about AI, are still close to daily work, and need to turn one confusing repetitive task into a useful workflow.**

This is the reader for every Reel. It is specific by role, capability level and job-to-be-done — not by a country Instagram cannot reliably target.

### Commercial geography later

Countries are a separate hypothesis for future partnerships, local cases, community, sales and services. Choose them after the account produces evidence, not before it has an audience.

## 3. Public positioning for the first content cycle

> **M2 Lab helps AI-curious SME owner-operators understand what AI can realistically do in their daily work — then turn one repetitive task into a useful workflow, without coding or chasing every new tool.**

### Editorial learning promise

> **Understand what matters. Choose one task. Build one useful workflow.**

The country is intentionally absent. The reader is not.

## 4. Who M2 Lab serves now

| Layer | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Primary reader** | Owner-operator of a roughly 2–50-person service SME; still owns or oversees marketing, sales, operations or client service; no dedicated AI team |
| **Adjacent reader** | Non-technical functional lead in that type of business who owns a repeatable process and can test a workflow |
| **Problem** | They know AI exists, but cannot tell which capability matters, which task to start with, or how to build a safe first workflow |
| **Outcome** | One understood, evaluated and useful workflow — not generic AI news or an unproven full automation promise |
| **Not for now** | Developers and advanced AI builders; general AI-news followers; virality or “make money with AI” seekers; high-risk regulated workflows |

## 5. Geography options as future commercial hypotheses

| Country / cluster | What it is good for | Why it is not the first public label |
|---|---|---|
| **Netherlands** | Strong candidate for English-working, internationally oriented and cross-border SMEs. The Dutch government says 90% of the population speaks English; 33% of enterprises with 10+ employees used AI in 2025. | English-accessible does not mean English-first for local domestic SME media. Do not claim to serve all Dutch SMEs without Dutch-language/local context. [Government.nl](https://www.government.nl/themes/international-cooperation/international-organisations/netherlands-as-host-country), [Eurostat](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/statistics-explained/SEPDF/cache/33473.pdf) |
| **Ireland** | The cleanest EU English-first market and an appropriate later country test. SME AI adoption rose from 8.0% in 2023 to 14.9% in 2024. | Small market: Ireland had 401,359 active enterprises in 2023. [Irish government report](https://enterprise.gov.ie/en/publications/publication-files/digital-decade-2025-ireland-country-report.PDF), [Irish CSO](https://www.cso.ie/en/releasesandpublications/ep/p-biiilb/businessinireland2023-insightsonthelifecycleofbusinesses/) |
| **Denmark / Sweden** | English works widely in business and the markets are digitally mature. | AI adoption is already 42% and 35% respectively among enterprises with 10+ employees, so the basic “where do I start?” angle may have less whitespace. Treat as a later test, not proof of low opportunity. [Eurostat](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/products-eurostat-news/w/ddn-20251211-2) |
| **UK** | Large native-English market. | The consultant’s saturation warning is material. A broad “AI for business” account is saturated there, and a country label does not solve that. Keep UK as a distribution and future-test market, not the account identity now. |
| **Estonia, Singapore, UAE** | Possible English-working or English-speaking niches. | Each needs a stronger local reason: Estonia is small; Singapore has a different time zone/context; UAE needs local business credibility. Do not add them to the first test. |

## 6. Recommended launch rule

For the first 24 posts, use **English-speaking / English-working European SME owner-operators** as the editorial audience. Do not name a country in the profile merely to look focused.

Use country references only when they make the post better. For example, a Netherlands-specific Reel needs a real Dutch case, a local collaborator, a local business situation or a locally meaningful search question — not only the word “Netherlands” in the cover.

## 7. How to select the first commercial geography with evidence

1. Publish 12 job-to-be-done-led posts without pretending they are local.
2. In Insights, record countries of reached accounts and new followers alongside saves, shares, profile visits and relevant comments/DMs.
3. After 12 posts, form a short list from actual signals. Do not treat views alone as a market signal.
4. For the strongest country candidate, make 3–4 genuinely local tests: case, collaborator, event, business norm or search phrasing.
5. Compare those tests with comparable non-local posts. If the country produces stronger qualified signals, it becomes the first commercial beachhead.
6. Until then, Netherlands is the best **research and collaboration hypothesis**; Ireland is the cleanest **English-first market hypothesis**. Neither should constrain the early account identity.

## 8. What this means for content production

The Content Engine should filter ideas by the reader’s job, not a guessed country:

> Would an English-speaking or English-working, non-technical SME owner-operator recognise this repeated task, learn something concrete about AI, and know the first next step?

Only after a country test begins should geography become a mandatory filter for that test series.
