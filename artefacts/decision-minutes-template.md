## Open (parked, with the question, not the discussion)
-

How to get there in five minutes: feed the transcript to the tool with this template and two examples of minutes you wrote yourself; ask it to fill the template only; read the decisions aloud at the end of the meeting so the room corrects them before they are written. Everything the tool adds that nobody said is deleted, and you note it, so the tool learns your format next time.
