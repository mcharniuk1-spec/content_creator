# M2-S05-02 — The meeting summary needs an unknown field

M2 Teardowns · English · 50 seconds · 9:16 · **Candidate; independent and owner review pending.**

**Viewer task:** Review a draft list of meeting actions. **Friction:** A fluent summary can hide missing ownership or dates. **Human decision:** The meeting owner confirms every action, owner and date.

## Three openings

1. A neat meeting summary can still invent the next step.
2. Who agreed to that deadline? Check before sharing the AI summary.
3. The most useful field in your meeting summary might be blank.

## Spoken script

A neat meeting summary can still invent the next step. Here is a made-up meeting note: check the room. Nobody has agreed who will do it. This deliberately faulty draft assigns a person and a deadline. Neither appears in the note. Ask for three fields: action, owner, and due date. Missing information should stay visibly unknown. Then compare each row with the original note. AI prepares the draft; the meeting owner checks it. If ownership is missing, ask the team. Do not let a tidy table make the decision. Only the confirmed version becomes the action list. Keep the source note beside it. Try this on a fictional note first: can you spot what was never agreed?
